Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Welcome to ClipLAN. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our peer-to-peer file sharing and clipboard application. Your privacy is paramount to us — which is precisely why we built ClipLAN to operate without any cloud infrastructure.

1. Information We Collect

None. ClipLAN operates entirely offline on your local network (LAN). We have zero servers, zero analytics, zero tracking pixels, and zero data collection infrastructure.

Specifically, ClipLAN does NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Files, content, or clipboard data you transfer
  • Location data or GPS coordinates

2. Local Network Operations

ClipLAN uses your device's local network capabilities exclusively for peer-to-peer functionality:

  • UDP Discovery: ClipLAN broadcasts small packets containing your device name on your local network to discover nearby devices. These packets never leave your local router.
  • TCP File Transfer: Files are streamed directly between devices on the same local network. No data is ever routed through external servers.
  • Clipboard Sharing: Text snippets are sent directly between devices via local broadcast or TCP unicast — never stored externally.

3. Device-Local Storage

To function properly, ClipLAN stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Device Name: A custom name you choose to identify yourself to peers on your network.
  • Transfer History: A local log of files you've sent or received (name, size, timestamp).
  • App Settings: Local preferences such as your default save directory and theme.

This data is stored using local on-device databases and is never transmitted to us or any third party.
